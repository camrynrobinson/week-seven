in my adventures on youtube yesterday i discovered the [Crocodile Tear channel](https://www.youtube.com/@CrocodileTear), which, according to the channel description, explores the following topics:
> "Investigations into World War II history:
-finding and identifying the bodies of missing soldiers,
-solving riddles and mysteries, correcting 80 year old mistakes,
-researching identification tags and named pieces of equipment (helmets, gas masks, canteens, etc),
-showing what research tools exist and how they can be used,
-promoting oral history (see my specialized channel "CrocodileTear Oral History Bank"),
-applying forensic methods to WWII issues,
-battlefield forensics, analysing battle damage and shrapnel
-connecting historical dots."

The channel belongs to Jean-Loup Gassend, a medical doctor and amateur historian from Switzerland (i think he is or was a researcher at Lausanne University Hospital!). His work focuses on forensic pathology and crime scene investigation, which he then applies to battlefield sites. He has worked on some absolutely fascinating projects and i want to compile his info somewhere so i can come back later.
